# Foundry: founder-led sales review

## Objective

Improve the quality and consistency of real sales conversations. The goal is not to automate outreach or manufacture urgency.

## Required pipeline fields

A prospect record should contain only what is needed:
- business / account identifier;
- segment and ICP fit;
- acquisition source and referral permission;
- buyer role and decision authority;
- problem trigger and current workaround;
- stage;
- last touch and next action;
- demo status;
- offer and price shown;
- objection category;
- outcome and lost reason.

Do not store sensitive customer or patient details in the sales record.

## Qualification

Prioritise evidence of:
- a recent recurring problem;
- a costly workaround or visible missed opportunity;
- an identifiable buyer and decision process;
- an existing budget or willingness to pay;
- ability to start a pilot soon;
- access to the relevant system/data;
- a clear success condition.

Do not qualify someone as a buyer because they say the product sounds interesting.

## Demo structure

1. Confirm the buyer's most recent example of the problem.
2. Show only the workflow that resolves that problem.
3. Use the buyer's approved, non-sensitive sample data or clearly labelled synthetic data.
4. Show failure boundaries, human control, handoff and what is not automated.
5. Confirm the value event and onboarding work required.
6. Present the offer, price and total expected costs clearly.
7. Ask for a concrete next step and date.

## Objection analysis

For each objection:
- preserve the buyer's exact words only when recording is permitted;
- classify as trust, switching cost, price, timing, missing capability, compliance, internal approval, or no urgency;
- separate the stated objection from the underlying interpretation;
- identify what evidence would resolve it;
- do not respond with an unsupported promise.

## Follow-up

- Send only after permission and within the channel's rules.
- A follow-up must reference the buyer's agreed next step, not create fake urgency.
- Stop when the buyer declines or opts out.
- Never automatically message referred contacts without their explicit permission.

## Weekly output

- prospects by stage and source;
- demos held and no-shows;
- trials started and activated;
- payments and lost reasons;
- objections repeated by multiple real buyers;
- next actions with owner and date;
- one change to the demo or offer, if evidence supports it.
