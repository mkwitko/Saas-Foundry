# Acquisition and Lead Intelligence

## Purpose

Find reachable buyers and repeatable acquisition channels for the specific SaaS project. The engine is not a bulk directory submitter, web scraper, or cold outreach bot.

## Pipeline

market_signal → channel_candidate → verified_channel → qualified_prospect → approved_campaign → outcome → learning

Each stage has a separate record and evidence requirements. A channel is not a lead; a lead is not a customer; a signup is not revenue.

## Channel catalogue

The curated discovery seeds are in catalog/acquisition-sources.yaml. Each source records its repository, license declaration, intended role, trust level, constraints and freshness policy.

The three seed repositories serve different roles:
- where-to-submit-your-startup: primary structured startup-directory discovery.
- awesome-startup-directories: broad channel and community discovery.
- PlacesToPostYourStartup: historical subreddit and website discovery.

Upstream records must be normalized and independently verified. Preserve licenses and attribution. Do not import upstream agent skills or execute scripts as part of catalogue ingestion.

## Prospect record

A prospect should include business name and website; segment, geography and buyer role if publicly established; fit rationale linked to ICP criteria; observed public trigger or need with source URL and date; public business contact page if available; acquisition source and confidence; status, next action and review date.

Do not harvest personal emails, private phone numbers, patient data or personal social profiles. Prefer public business channels and warm, permission-based referrals. Keep the shortlist small enough for meaningful research.

## Channel scorecard

Evaluate each channel on ICP concentration and buyer intent; evidence quality and freshness; time to first qualified conversation; cash cost and founder effort; platform, legal and reputational risk; measurability and attribution; ability to repeat.

Do not rank channels by domain authority, follower count, traffic estimate or low CPL alone. The meaningful outcome is qualified conversations and paid customers.

## SEO and content

Prioritize high-intent commercial queries, pages that answer a real buyer question better than current results, proof and trust content the business can substantiate, technical indexing and conversion issues, and content that can be maintained.

Avoid mass-produced thin pages, fabricated comparisons, invented reviews and unsupported product claims.

## Reddit and communities

Use communities to understand buyer language, repeated pain and channel rules. Read current rules before recommending participation. A useful answer is not automatically permission to promote. Do not automate unsolicited DMs, repetitive promotional posts or prohibited scraping.

## Paid acquisition

Do not activate campaigns without a validated offer and landing page; working conversion event and attribution; explicit target segment and geography; budget, maximum loss and stop condition; creative and claim review; owner approval.

## Approval gates

Human approval is mandatory before outreach, posting, submission, paid listing, ad spend, publishing, or production changes. Prepare drafts and execution checklists; do not perform the external action silently.

## First use on ia-chat

For the initial Brazilian SaaS aimed at small businesses and independent professionals, prioritize referrals and permission-based introductions; direct conversations with carefully qualified business owners; relevant professional partnerships and associations; high-intent search and useful SEO pages; selected directories as secondary low-cost experiments; paid search only after conversion instrumentation and offer validation.

Do not prioritize global startup launch directories over channels that reach the actual buyer.
